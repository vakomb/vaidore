What data we process

To fulfil your order - your name, email address, phone number and delivery address. Payment card details are processed only by Stripe - we never receive or store them.

Bag and favourites

Your bag and favourite products are stored only in your browser (localStorage) and are not sent to us. You can delete them by clearing your browser data.

Language choice and cookie

The website is available in Lithuanian and English. We remember your chosen language in your browser (localStorage) and in the functional cookie "vaidore_lang" (value "lt" or "en", valid for 1 year), so that the website opens in the same language next time and is not switched automatically. On your first visit, the language may be chosen based on the country determined from your IP address (the IP address itself is not stored). This cookie is necessary for a feature you have requested, is not used for tracking or advertising, and is not shared with anyone. You can delete it by clearing your browser cookies.

Who we share data with

Our payment service provider Stripe and parcel carriers (Omniva, DPD) - only as much as is needed to fulfil your order. The website is hosted by Vercel.

How long we keep data

Order data is kept for as long as required by accounting legislation (up to 10 years).
